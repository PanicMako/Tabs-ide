# Getting real reach for Tabs

## The honest part first

A post from a one-month-old account with 0 followers almost never reaches 100K views on its own. When small accounts go viral, it's nearly always because **someone bigger reshared, quoted or replied to them**, or because a Reddit or HN post blew up and sent people over. So the plan is:

1. Make the content as shareable as possible (mostly the video).
2. Put it in front of people who already have an audience.
3. Take many shots, not one. Every clip is another chance.

## Should you pay for X Premium?

- **What's actually documented:** Premium gives your **replies priority** under other people's posts. X lists this on its Premium page.
- **What isn't proven:** claims of a "2–4x" or "10x" reach boost for your own posts come from marketing blogs, and they contradict each other. One analysis of X's published ranking code found no Premium multiplier for the For You feed.
- **Verdict:** worth it for **1–2 months**, mainly because replying under bigger accounts is your main growth tactic, and Premium puts those replies higher. It also lets you upload longer, higher-quality video. Cancel if nothing changes.
- **Grok / SuperGrok is a different thing.** It's the AI chatbot subscription and does nothing for reach. Don't buy that for this.

## What moves the needle, in order

### 1. The video (biggest factor)
- Make a **15–25 second cut** alongside the 51-second one. Short clips get watched to the end, and X pushes videos that people finish.
- The **first 2 seconds** have to show something: a cluttered Mac with windows everywhere, then *snap*, one Tabs window, then clicking between project tabs.
- Burn in captions or big on-screen text ("my mac before" → "after"). Most people scroll with the sound off.

### 2. Borrow bigger audiences (this is where virality comes from)
- **Theo:** he has publicly said he likes elaborate T3 Code forks. When he posts about T3 Code, reply with a genuine one-liner and your clip ("built a fork that gives each project its own tab with editor + browser + terminal, here's how it looks"). Don't ask him to retweet. Just be visible and good.
- **Claude Code / Codex / Cursor threads:** when someone complains about juggling windows, terminals or too many agents, reply helpfully, and include the clip only if it actually answers their problem.
- Reply to **5–10 posts a day** in your niche with real, specific replies. Premium boosts exactly this.

### 3. Post it in X Communities
Join a few relevant **Communities** (e.g. "Build in Public", indie dev, AI coding ones) and share the post there. Community posts reach members who don't follow you.

### 4. Hit other channels with the same video
Each channel is a separate chance at a spike, and each spike sends people to your X and GitHub.
- **Hacker News (Show HN):** often the single biggest traffic source for open source dev tools. Write the post yourself; HN asks that it not be AI-written. Link the GitHub repo.
- **Reddit:** your r/SideProject post once the mods approve it, then the others in the playbook.
- **YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels:** upload the same short clip. Dev tool shorts can travel far on their own.
- **LinkedIn:** the story post. This is where recruiters actually are.
- **Newsletters:** submit Tabs to dev tool newsletters and roundups (most have a "submit a tool" form), plus "awesome" lists on GitHub (awesome-claude-code, awesome-electron, etc.).
- **dev.to / Hashnode:** "what I learned turning a T3 Code fork into a full workspace". It's searchable for years, and good for job hunting.

### 5. Keep posting (most launches die here)
After the launch, post a **new 10–20 s clip every 2–3 days**, one feature each:
- the Quick Actions wheel (⌘⌥K)
- pinning Figma or Vercel to the toolbar
- Relay saving your context for tomorrow
- switching between 3 projects in 2 seconds
- a bug someone reported and you fixed ("fixed in v2.3.3, thanks @…")

Each one is a fresh shot at a reshare. One of them taking off is far more likely than the first post doing it.

### 6. The first hour after any post
Reply to everyone, fast. Early replies are one of the strongest signals X uses. Post when your audience is awake: Tue–Thu, 7:30–9:30 pm IST (US morning).

## For the job goal

- Put **"open to front-end / full-stack roles"** in your bio *now*. You don't need to wait until it's big. Recruiters judge you on the project and how you talk about it, not your follower count.
- Pin the thread, link GitHub on your profile, and have the profile README up (still paused).
- Keep a simple log: downloads, GitHub stars, issues, nice quotes. "Shipped an open source desktop app used by N people" is a strong résumé line at any N.
