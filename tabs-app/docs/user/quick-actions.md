# Quick Actions and the blank browser

Press **⌘ ⌥ K** (or **Ctrl Alt K**) to open Quick Actions: jump to a tool, or open a throwaway
browser or terminal.

## Curved menus

Sub-menus can follow the edge of the ring, or appear as a plain card. Change this in
**Settings → Quick Actions → Curved menus**.

## Blank browser

A blank browser opens from Quick Actions or the Browser menu. Under **Settings → Quick Actions →
Blank browser** you can choose:

- **Start page**: Blank, Google, DuckDuckGo, or a custom address.
- **Keep browser controls open**: a blank page always shows its address bar, back, forward and
  reload so you can start typing.
