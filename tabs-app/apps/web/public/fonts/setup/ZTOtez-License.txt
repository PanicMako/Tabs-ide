ZT Otez is a refined serif typeface crafted with elegance, precision, and timeless character. Inspired by classic editorial lettering and 20th-century French book typography, ZT Otez is designed for those who seek both beauty and performance in every letter.

With a stunning 90 styles in total — 45 upright and 45 italic — ZT Otez ranges from Thin Condensed to Black Expanded, making it exceptionally flexible for everything from luxury branding and magazine layouts to packaging and poster design.

What makes ZT Otez unique?

- 90 Styles: 45 Roman + 45 Italic

- Weight range: Thin to Black

- Width range: Condensed to Expanded

- Extreme 28° italic slant

- Support for 156+ languages

- Ideal for branding, headlines, editorial, and high-end packaging

Its italic styles are dramatically slanted at 28°, giving a unique, high-energy feel that complements the poised structure of the roman weights. Each style is carefully drawn with attention to typographic grace and multilingual support, making ZT Otez a timeless serif you’ll reach for again and again.
